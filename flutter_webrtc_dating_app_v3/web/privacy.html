1. Who we are

Destined is a free dating and chat app operated by an independent developer based in India. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the party responsible for (the "controller" or "data fiduciary" of) the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at test@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password (if you sign up with email), or your basic Google account details (name, email address and profile picture) if you sign in with Google. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Profile information: your name or display name, date of birth, gender, the gender(s) you are interested in, a short bio, and any other details you choose to add to your profile.
  • Photos and avatar: photos you upload to your profile, or an avatar generated by the App for you.
  • Questionnaire and astrology: your answers to the compatibility questionnaire, and astrology details we work out from your date of birth (for example, your zodiac sign) to suggest compatible matches.
  • Messages and media: the text messages, voice notes and images you send in 1:1 chats, and in-chat game moves and results.
  • Safety reports: reports you submit about other members, including the reason and any details you add, and the list of members you have blocked.
  • Support requests: the content of any email you send us, together with your email address and the technical details included in a support email (app version, platform and your user ID).

Information collected when you use the App

  • Location: if you allow it, we use your device location to find people near you. We round your position to roughly 1 km and also convert it into a coarse area code (a "geohash", covering a few kilometres). The rounded position is stored only on your own account record; the area code is what discovery uses. We never store a precise street address. Other members only see an approximate distance (for example "5 km away"), never your exact location. You can turn location access off in your device settings; discovery may then be less accurate.
  • Discovery settings and activity: your discovery preferences (such as age range and distance), likes, passes, matches, and when you were last active or online.
  • Call information: when a call starts and ends, who it was with, and whether it was answered or missed. We do not record the audio or video of your calls (see section 6).
  • Device and push information: a push notification identifier for your device, your device type and operating system, app version, and notification preferences.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: if the App crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects a crash report, which includes device model, operating system version, app version, the technical details of the error and a pseudonymous identifier.
  • App integrity data: Firebase App Check checks that requests come from a genuine copy of the App on a genuine device, to protect our services from abuse. This uses device attestation services provided by Google (Play Integrity) and Apple (App Attest / DeviceCheck).

Sensitive information

Some of the information you share on a dating app can reveal sensitive things about you, such as your sexual orientation (through the genders you are interested in) or your beliefs. You choose whether to share this. We only use it to provide the App to you, for example to show you the right people, and we never use it for advertising.

4. What other members can see

Destined is a social app, so some information is visible to other members by design:

  • Your public profile: display name, age (not your full date of birth), gender, photos or avatar, bio, zodiac sign and compatibility hints, and your approximate distance.
  • Your online or last-active status.
  • The messages, voice notes, images and game moves you send to a match, which that person can read, save or screenshot. Please think before you share.

Your email address, password, exact location and full date of birth are never shown to other members.

5. Who we share your information with

We share information only with service providers ("processors") who help us run the App, and only as needed for that purpose. They are not allowed to use your information for their own purposes.

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in (Firebase Authentication), our databases (Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database), file storage (Cloud Storage), server functions (Cloud Functions), crash reporting (Crashlytics) and abuse protection (App Check). See Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Cloudinary (Cloudinary Ltd.): stores and delivers images and media you upload. See Cloudinary's privacy policy.
  • OneSignal (OneSignal, Inc.): delivers push notifications to your device. See OneSignal's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.): runs a small server (a Cloudflare Worker) that passes notification requests to OneSignal. It handles your push identifier and the short notification text, and does not store your messages. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Connection servers for calls: calls connect directly between devices, but to get through some networks they use standard connection servers (STUN servers run by Google, and TURN relay servers, which may be run by a third-party relay provider). These servers see network (IP) addresses and may relay encrypted call data; they cannot see or hear the content of the call.
  • Apple and Google app stores: when you download the App, the stores process information under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information:

  • if we are required to by law, court order or a valid request from a government or law enforcement authority;
  • to protect the safety of any person, to investigate fraud or abuse, or to enforce our Terms of Service;
  • to a buyer or successor if the App is ever transferred, in which case this policy will continue to protect your information, or you will be told before it changes.

6. Calls, chat and on-device processing

  • Audio and video calls use WebRTC, a peer-to-peer technology. The audio and video travel encrypted between your device and the other person's device. We do not record, store or have access to the content of your calls. Because calls are peer-to-peer, the two devices exchange network (IP) addresses to connect, so the person you call may be able to see your IP address, which can reveal an approximate area. Only call people you trust.
  • Video filters (such as beauty filters) are applied on your device. The unfiltered video is not sent to us.
  • Translation of chat messages uses Google ML Kit on-device translation. The translation model is downloaded to your device and messages are translated on your device; the message text is not sent to us or to Google for translation.
  • Chat messages, voice notes and images are stored on our providers' servers (Firebase and Cloudinary) so they can be delivered and shown in your chat history. They are encrypted in transit and at rest by those providers, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. We do not read your messages, except when a message has been reported to us or where we need to investigate a safety or legal issue.

7. How long we keep your information

  • While your account is active, we keep your profile, matches and chats so the App works.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, voice notes stored under your account, sign-in account, likes, blocks, game data and push identifier. Messages you already sent stay visible in the other person's chat, shown as from a "Deleted user", because they are also part of that person's conversation.
  • Temporary data such as call signalling and incoming-call records is deleted automatically, normally within minutes to a few hours.
  • Safety reports and records of accounts removed for breaking our rules may be kept for as long as needed to protect the community, prevent the same person returning, and meet legal obligations, generally no longer than 3 years after the account is closed.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Backups and logs held by our providers may keep copies for a short period (usually up to 30 to 60 days) before they are overwritten.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request, and then up to 2 years.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the GDPR in the EU/UK), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date. You can edit most of your profile directly in the App.
  • Delete your information. You can delete your account in the App at any time (see section 9).
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, for example by turning off location or notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Object to or restrict certain uses, and ask for your data in a portable format where the law provides this.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so (India).
  • Complain to us first, and then, if you are not satisfied, to your local data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).

To use any of these rights, email test@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We may need to confirm your identity. We will reply within 30 days, and we will not charge you for a reasonable request.

9. Deleting your account

Open the App, go to Settings → Delete account and follow the steps. For your security you will be asked to sign in again first. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you cannot access the App, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), encryption at rest by our providers, database security rules that limit who can read each piece of data, App Check to block requests that do not come from the genuine App, and limited access to production systems. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and keep your device secure. If we become aware of a data breach that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

11. International transfers

We are based in India, and our service providers (such as Google, Cloudinary, OneSignal and Cloudflare) may store and process your information in other countries, including the United States and countries in the European Union. These countries may have different data protection laws from yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as our providers' standard contractual clauses, to protect your information when it is transferred.

12. Adults only (18+)

Destined is only for adults aged 18 or older. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and do not allow anyone under 18 to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we will close the account and delete their information. If you believe a minor is using Destined, please report the profile in the App or email us.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add new features or the law changes. We will change the effective date at the top, and if the changes are significant we will let you know in the App or by email before they take effect. If you keep using the App after the changes take effect, the updated policy will apply.
